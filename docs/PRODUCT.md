# Product

A fictional UAE spare-parts distributor reference app for catalogue, inventory, purchasing and sales operations. Demo numbers and vehicle compatibility are never authoritative. Desktop-first with responsive navigation and horizontally scrollable tables.

Modules: Dashboard; Catalogue (Parts, Part Numbers, Cross References, Brands, Categories, Vehicle Compatibility); Inventory (Stock, Warehouses, Transfers, Adjustments, Low Stock); Purchasing (Suppliers, Purchase Orders, Goods Receiving); Sales (Customers, Quotations, Sales Orders, Invoices, Returns); Reports; Documents; Management (Branches, Users, Roles, Settings, Audit Log).
